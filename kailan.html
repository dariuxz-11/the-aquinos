When Did the History of the Aquino Family Begin?

The Aquino family is one of the well-known political families in the Philippines, originating from the province of Tarlac. Their prominence in society began with General Servillano “Mianong” Aquino, who was born on April 20, 1874, in Angeles, Pampanga. He became a general during the Philippine Revolution against the Spaniards and served as a delegate to the Malolos Congress in 1898.

The family's influence grew further when his son, Benigno Aquino Sr., entered politics. He was elected as the representative of the Second District of Tarlac and later became a senator. He also served as the Speaker of the National Assembly during the Second Philippine Republic. Because of his government positions and connections with prominent families in Tarlac, the Aquino family became increasingly recognized in politics.

Their political influence continued through Benigno “Ninoy” Aquino Jr., who served as the mayor of Concepcion, governor of Tarlac, and senator. He became well-known for opposing the administration of Ferdinand Marcos and the implementation of Martial Law. On August 21, 1983, he was assassinated upon his return to the Philippines, and his death strengthened the movement against dictatorship.

After his death, his wife, Corazon “Cory” Aquino, became an important leader in Philippine history. She led the opposition during the 1986 People Power Revolution, which resulted in the end of Marcos's rule. She was elected as the 11th President of the Philippines and served from 1986 to 1992.

The family's political influence continued through their son, Benigno “Noynoy” Aquino III, who served as a congressman, senator, and the 15th President of the Philippines from 2010 to 2016. Because several members of the family held high positions in government, the Aquino family became one of the most recognized political dynasties in the Philippines.





They Gained Power and Influence Through:

  1. Historical Legacy

    – Their family name first became prominent through Servillano Aquino's participation in the Philippine Revolution.

  2. Entry into Politics

    – Benigno Aquino Sr. gained the trust of voters and held important positions in the government.

  3. Connections with Powerful Families

    – Their influence was also strengthened through their connection with the Cojuangco family.

  4. Fight for Democracy

    – Ninoy and Cory Aquino became symbols of resistance against dictatorship.

  5. Continued Government Service

    – Their family's political legacy continued through Noynoy Aquino and other relatives.




Conclusion

Overall, the influence of the Aquino family began with their role in Philippine history and grew stronger through their involvement in politics, connections with other prominent families, and participation in significant events in the country. They became well-known not only because of their political power but also because of their contributions and role in shaping the history of democracy in the Philippines.
